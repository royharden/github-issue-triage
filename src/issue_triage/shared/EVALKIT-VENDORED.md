seals and durable exposure history remain separate. A matching hash/history callback or synthetic
control proves none of those. No S6 sealed data is released or evaluated by this adoption.

Python3.12/Pydantic2 are required. No provider, SDK, OCR/image engine or live billing is introduced.
The application still reports evaluations as not_yet_measured until its actual authorized pipeline
and domain adapter run. This is no finished-app or model-quality acceptance.
